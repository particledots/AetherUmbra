1d. Supersaw (StereoSuperSaw.h)
   The header says "Adapted from" the 2010 bachelor thesis "How to Emulate the Super Saw" by Adam Szabo (KTH Royal
   Institute of Technology, Stockholm). What the firmware takes from it are numbers: the seven detune offsets (for example
   0.11002313, 0.06288439, 0.01952356, 0.01991221, 0.06216538, 0.10745242) and the two mix curves (-0.73764 x^2 + 1.2841 x
   + 0.044372 and -0.55366 x + 0.99785), which the thesis measured from a Roland JP-8000. Measured values and curve
   fits are facts, not text or code. The code around them is Befaco's own (GPL). Not checked: the thesis document itself
   could not be opened in the workspace (the fetch tool refused the KTH and web.archive.org addresses), so whether it
   carries a copyright or licence line was not read. The thesis contains no program code that is used here. The thesis is
   credited here and in the README. "Roland" and "JP-8000" are trademarks of Roland Corporation; they are named only to
   say what the thesis measured.
